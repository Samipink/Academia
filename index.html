My name is Samantha Mulholland and I am a Senior Software Engineer at Sciencesoft Ltd where I have worked since 2014. I work mainly WPF using the MVVM pattern in a C# environment as well as legacy products written in VB6. I extensively use AI e.g. in planning, implementation and most importantly testing. Part of my duties include designing and managing the company website, creating all required artwork for conference stalls, posters, mailers and E-cards as well as all icon sets and graphics required for the Sciencesoft product suite.

I started work at Sciencesoft Ltd after successfully completing an MPhil in computing science at the University of Glasgow. This research was sponsored by Sciencesoft Ltd where I looked at novel ways of compressing and visualising 3D oil reservoir grid information. Prior to this I studied at Glasgow Caledonian University where I obtained a Masters Degree in 3D Design for Virtual Environments and a first class Honours Degree in Internet Software Development. I started University life as a mature student with only a few years of computing background obtained while studying at Glasgow Metropolitan College, now the City of Glasgow College when I enrolled on a Woman's management course in 2004. I later successfully completed a 2 year Basic IT course before enroling in University.
