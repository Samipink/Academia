I have several hobbies and interests but one of them is motorbikes. I remember when I was around 12 years old, during the summer holidays I would go with my friend down to the local scrambling course. It was really just a section of rough grass and small hills where my friend's older brother and his mates would take their motorbikes. I used to happily hang on to the back of my friend's brother's bike as he went round the course and over the small make-shift jumps. I never really thought much of it until my mid-life-crisis when I thought I should and see about having some motorbike lessons purely just for a bit of fun and something new to learn.

Suzuki Vstrom DL1050XT
That was about 12 years ago now. I enroled on a CBTcourse. This was supposed to be a 1 day course where you have some basic training in the morning then taken out on a supervised run in the afternoon. It took me 3 attempts before they would take me out on the road but eventually I got there. I bought myself a wee 125cc which I spent most of my lunchbreaks and weekends riding around on.

Honda 125 CBF
I decided to go for my A1 licence and over the next year successfully passed the 3 stages. I then progressed to larger bikes starting with my first big bike, a Kawasaki Ninja 600.
